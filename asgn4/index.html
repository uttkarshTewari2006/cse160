Assignment 4: Lighting and OBJ

Note to TA: This submission was built from scratch, so it uses a small lit scene instead of reusing the previous animal/world project. The scene includes the required cube, multiple spheres, point light, spotlight, normal visualization, Phong shading, and loaded OBJ model.

Use W A S D to move and Q E to turn the camera. The point light can orbit or be moved with sliders, and the spotlight follows the camera like a flashlight.

Please use a browser that supports canvas.
